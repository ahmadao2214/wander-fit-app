# The account comes after the program reveal

The First Run asks for an account only after the athlete has answered intake and seen their own program: the real Day 1, the GPP/SPP/SSP bar and the session count up to the season. Before this, sign-up came first and the athlete answered nine screens before seeing anything of their own. The decision follows Apple's guidance to delay sign-in as long as possible, and Duolingo's move of sign-up to after the first lesson.

Until sign-up, the intake answers live only on the phone, and the reveal reads data that needs no account (the sports list and the workout templates). Answers stay on the device if the athlete leaves, so they resume where they stopped, and are cleared once the program is saved. The phone also records the first-launch time and sends it with the intake, so the time from first launch to Day 1 can be measured.

The account sheet has no skip: the program is saved together with the account, so no account exists without an intake, and accounts left in that state by the old flow are removed rather than supported. If the athlete signs in to an account that already has a program, the existing program stays and the new answers are dropped with a short notice, because replacing it would overwrite real training history. The app is athlete-only, so the sign-up step asks no role question.

## Considered options

- **Account first, as before.** Simplest, since every intake answer could be saved to the server as it is given, but the athlete gives an email and a code before getting anything back.
- **No account at all until a later session.** Rejected because Day 1's progress, and the day-2 return, need the program saved to an account.
