# Founder facts

- Product: Wander-Fit, an iOS app (Expo/React Native). Athlete picks a sport, enters experience and weeks until the season; app generates a 3-phase periodized offseason program (general prep, specific prep, sport-specific prep) with 1RM-based intensity scaling, a calendar and workout logging. (2026-10-03, competitor-matrix)
- Team: one engineer and one certified personal trainer. (2026-10-03, competitor-matrix)
- Time building: seven months. (2026-10-03, competitor-matrix)
- Real users: zero. (2026-10-03, competitor-matrix)
- Problem hypothesis: coaches hand out offseason programs and learn in the first 2 to 3 weeks of preseason who skipped, through soft-tissue injuries and poor conditioning. (2026-10-03, competitor-matrix)
- Likely buyer (founder's hypothesis): high school, club, or D2/D3 coach without a strength and conditioning staff. (2026-10-03, competitor-matrix)
- Northwestern athletic trainer told the founders: athletes get 4 separate sheets; adherence is low even at the pro level; completion checkboxes can't verify the work; RPE x minutes gives an acute:chronic workload ratio, healthy at 0.8 to 1.3; training age should be separate from age. (2026-10-03, competitor-matrix)
