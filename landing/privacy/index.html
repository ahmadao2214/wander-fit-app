Privacy Policy

Pilot version 2026-11-22. This is a working draft for the WanderFit pilot. An attorney will review it before public launch.

WanderFit ("we", "us") provides the WanderFit strength and conditioning app for iPhone, Android and the web, and this website (together, the "Services"). This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, who processes it for us, and the choices athletes and parents or legal guardians have.

1. Who can use WanderFit, and athletes 14 to 17

  • Minimum age 14. WanderFit is for athletes aged 14 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13.
  • Age check. When you first set up the app we ask for your birth month and year. We use it to check that you are old enough and to place you in a training age group (14–17, 18–35 or 36+). We ask for the month, not the day, so the check is accurate to the month. The birth month and year stay on your device and are not saved on our servers.
  • Private by default. WanderFit has no public profiles, social feeds, leaderboards or messaging between athletes.
  • No ads. We do not sell personal information, show third-party ads, or track you across other apps. We do not use the advertising identifier (IDFA).
  • Parents and guardians. If you are the parent or legal guardian of an athlete aged 14 to 17 and want to see or delete their account, or you believe a child under 13 has given us personal information, send a request through the support form. We will check that the request is genuine and then act on it.

2. What we collect

Account

  • First name, shown in your profile.
  • Email address, used for sign-in, account checks and replies to your requests.
  • An account ID and your sign-in method (email, Sign in with Apple, or Sign in with Google).
  • When you accepted the terms and which version, and, for athletes 14 to 17, when you confirmed a parent or guardian's permission.

Training information you give us

  • Intake answers: your sport, your season or tryout date (or "No set season"), training days per week, age group, and training experience.
  • Program and workouts: your three-phase program (GPP, SPP, SSP), schedule changes, logged exercises, sets, reps, weights, duration, effort ratings, optional workout notes, optional one-rep maxes for the core lifts, check-in answers, and when you finished each workout.
  • Feedback: bug reports, feature requests and ratings you send from inside the app.

This website

  • Beta sign-up form: your email, whether you are an athlete 14–17, an adult athlete, or a parent, an optional parent email, the season date you picked, and your plan preference. We use it to send beta access and to learn which plan people prefer.
  • Support form: your email, the kind of request, and your message.
  • Form limits: when you send either form we record your IP address and email to count requests over a 15-minute window, so one person cannot flood the form. A cleanup that runs every minute removes the record after the window ends.

Crash reports and usage analytics

  • Crash reports: when the app crashes we receive the technical error, the app version, and the type of device and operating system, tagged with your sign-in account ID. Crash reports do not include your name or email.
  • Usage analytics: we record events such as opening, starting or finishing a workout, tagged with your WanderFit account ID. They do not include your name or email, and IP addresses are discarded. You can turn this off in the app under Profile → App settings → Privacy → "Share anonymous usage analytics".
  • Screen replays: the web app can record masked replays of a session to help us test, with all text hidden. It is offered only on the web, only to accounts in an adult age group (18–35 or 36+), and only records after you switch it on. It is turned off for everyone during the pilot.

What we do not collect

  • Precise location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone data, Apple Health or Health Connect data, or advertising identifiers.
  • Your full birth date.

3. How we use information

We use the information above only to:

  1. create and sign you in to your account;
  2. build, personalize and save your training program and workout history;
  3. keep your schedule and progress in sync across your devices;
  4. find and fix crashes, and understand which parts of the app are used;
  5. answer support, privacy and account deletion requests; and
  6. keep the Services secure and meet legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information and do not share it for advertising.

5. How long we keep data, and deleting your account

We keep your account and training records while your account is active. You can permanently delete your account and its data at any time:

  1. In the app: Profile → App settings → Delete account.
  2. Without the app: follow the steps on the delete your account page.

Deleting your account removes your sign-in record from Clerk and your profile, intake answers, program, one-rep maxes, schedule and workout records from Convex. It cannot be undone.

A request sent through the support form, including a deletion request, is kept so that we have a record of what we were asked. Once a request is closed and 90 days have passed since you sent it, a daily cleanup deletes it.

6. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). On iPhone and Android, sign-in tokens are kept in secure device storage (iOS Keychain or Android Keystore). Our servers check that each athlete can read only their own records. No online service is free of risk, so use a strong password and protect your device.

7. Cookies and local storage

The app and the web app store what is needed to keep you signed in and to hold your set-up answers before you create an account. The web app keeps its analytics ID in memory only, so it is gone when you close the tab. This website stores your beta sign-up state in your browser and uses no advertising or tracking cookies.

8. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • know and see the personal information we hold about you;
  • correct information that is wrong;
  • ask us to delete your personal information; and
  • not be treated differently for using these rights.

To use any of these rights, send a request through the support form.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as WanderFit changes. When we make an important change we will update the version date at the top and tell you in the app or by email.

10. Contact us

For privacy questions, parent or guardian requests, and general support, use the support form.
