Delete your WanderFit account

You can permanently delete your WanderFit account and all of its training data at any time.

Option 1: in the app (fastest)

  1. Open WanderFit and sign in.
  2. Tap the Profile tab.
  3. Tap App settings.
  4. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Option 2: without the app

Use this if you no longer have the app, or if you are the parent or legal guardian of an athlete aged 14 to 17.

  1. Open the support form and choose "Delete an account".
  2. Enter the email on the account. A parent or guardian should enter their own email, and put the athlete's account email and their relationship in the message.
  3. We reply by email to check the request is genuine, then delete the account within 7 business days.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in details and account ID, held by Clerk.
  • Your profile (first name and email), intake answers, program, one-rep maxes, schedule changes, check-ins and logged workouts, held by Convex.

What is kept

  • No training or account data is kept once deletion is complete.
  • A deletion request sent through the support form, with the email and message in it, is kept as a record of the request. We delete it within 90 days of closing it.
  • Crash reports and usage events are tagged with account IDs, not your name or email, and can no longer be linked to you after deletion.
  • If paid plans are offered later, billing records may be kept where the law requires it.

Store subscriptions

Deleting your WanderFit account does not cancel a subscription billed by Apple or Google. If you have one, cancel it in your Apple or Google account settings.
