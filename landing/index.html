Questions parents and athletes ask.

Is strength training safe for 14 to 17 year old athletes?

Yes. Youth programs scale to the Youth (14–17) age group and open with GPP to build general fitness and movement quality before heavier sport-specific work. WanderFit does not ask for a day-one max lift.

What equipment do I need?

Current workouts assume standard gym equipment such as dumbbells, barbells, and benches. A home and bodyweight equipment option is on our roadmap.

How do the 2-week free beta and pricing work?

The 2-week beta is free with no credit card required. We ask your plan preference in the form to understand whether athletes and parents prefer a one-time $35 Season Pass or $15/month once paid plans launch.

How is WanderFit different from asking ChatGPT for a workout plan?

ChatGPT gives you a one-off text list. WanderFit maps your season date across three phases (GPP, SPP, and SSP) with Intro, Build, Peak, and Deload weeks, guided set-by-set logging, and real session history. Optional AI-assistant compatibility is also in progress.

I am over 18 or do not have an upcoming tryout. Can I still try it?

Yes. Choose Adult Athlete (18+) in the sign-up form, then pick "No set season" and Performance (18–35) or Vitality (36+) during your First Run. You will receive a 12-week program with four weeks in each phase.
