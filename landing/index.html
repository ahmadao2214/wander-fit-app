Fit for your sport. New to the bar.

Years of practice don’t tell an app what you can squat. So WanderFit doesn’t ask for a max lift on day one. If you’ve never lifted, you start with two weeks of learning the movements, with weight you can control.

Your practices and games count. A heavy week with the team means a lighter week in the gym, so you show up to the test fresh instead of sore.

Questions parents ask.

Is lifting safe for teens?

Well-coached strength training is widely recommended for teen athletes. The risk is too much weight too soon, which is why new lifters start with technique and light loads.

We don’t have a gym.

The first two weeks need a pair of dumbbells or nothing at all. Tell us what you have and the plan works around it.

What if the tryout moves?

Change the date and the plan rebuilds from today. The easy week always lands right before the test.
