Refund Policy

Pilot version 2026-11-22. This is a working draft for the WanderFit pilot. An attorney will review it before public launch.

1. Free pilot and beta

The pilot and beta are free. We do not collect payment or card details, so there are no charges to refund.

2. Purchases through the Apple App Store

If we later offer a paid plan and you buy it through the App Store, Apple takes the payment and handles billing and refunds under its own policies.

  • To ask Apple for a refund, go to reportaproblem.apple.com and sign in with the Apple Account you used to buy.
  • To manage or cancel a subscription, open iOS Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions.

3. Purchases through Google Play

If you buy a paid plan through Google Play, Google takes the payment and handles refunds under its own policies. To ask for a refund or manage a subscription, go to your Google Play subscriptions or contact Google Play support.

4. Purchases on the web

We do not sell anything on the web yet. If we start to, the refund terms for those purchases will be added here before the first sale.

5. Deleting your account does not cancel store billing

Deleting your WanderFit account erases your account and training history. It does not cancel a subscription billed by Apple or Google. Cancel that in your Apple or Google account settings.

6. Questions

For help with billing, use the support form.
